Deleting your Sister Vuo data

Last updated: 20 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the messages and phone number connected to your use of Sister Vuo.

How to ask

  1. Open an issue at github.com/220404773/sister-vuo/issues with the title "Data deletion request".
  2. Do not put your full phone number in the issue, because it is public. Write only the last four digits and the date you used the service.
  3. Say that you want a private reply. We will tell you how to confirm that the number is yours before we delete anything.

What we delete

  • The saved transcripts of your sessions. You can also ask us to delete a session's transcript on request.
  • Any record linking you to a session.

Automatic deletion

Transcript files are deleted automatically after the retention period, which is 30 days by default. The operator can configure a shorter period, or keep transcripts until they are removed manually.

What we cannot delete

  • Messages held by WhatsApp (Meta). Use WhatsApp's own settings for those.
  • Anything held by the AI model provider under its own terms.

How long it takes

We aim to confirm your request within 14 days.

See also the Privacy Policy.
