Sister Vuo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Sister Vuo is an AI patient simulator for nursing education. A student nurse chats with a fictional patient, then receives feedback on the conversation. This page explains what happens to the messages you send.

Please do not share real patient information

Every patient in Sister Vuo is fictional. Do not type the name, phone number, address, ID number or health details of any real person, including yourself.

What we collect

  • Your messages to the simulator, and the patient replies and feedback it generates.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number, if you use the WhatsApp version. It is used to keep your chat in one session and to send replies to you.
  • A name, only if you type one into the web version.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or voice notes. Messages that are not text are answered with a request to send text only.

How we use it

  • To run the simulation and give you scored feedback.
  • To check that the simulator works safely and fairly, and to fix problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles your messages

  • WhatsApp (Meta) delivers messages between you and Sister Vuo and has its own privacy policy.
  • An AI model provider receives the conversation so that it can write the patient's replies and the feedback. We currently use Llama models through OpenRouter. The provider's own terms apply to what it receives.

How long we keep it

  • Active sessions are kept in memory and removed after 120 minutes without activity.
  • A transcript of each session is saved to a file on the operator's computer or server. In these logs the phone number is shortened to its last four digits. These files are not deleted automatically. They are removed when you ask us to delete them, or when the operator clears them.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to delete it. See how to request deletion. If you are in South Africa, you also have rights under POPIA, including the right to complain to the Information Regulator.

Security

Webhook requests from WhatsApp are verified before they are accepted, and the web interface can be protected with an access key. No system is perfectly secure, which is another reason to keep real personal details out of your messages.

Who to contact

This is a small education project. To ask a question or make a request, open an issue at github.com/220404773/sister-vuo/issues. Please do not put personal details in a public issue; say that you want a private reply and we will tell you how.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Sister Vuo is for education only. Its feedback is not a formal assessment and is not clinical advice.
